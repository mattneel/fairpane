## Stop-rule observations

### Case 1 on the base

Case 1 did not fail with `error.OutOfMemory` on the base.
It failed with `error.TestUnexpectedResult` at its floor expectation `freeEventSlots(engine) >= engine.requests.count()` (`raw/tests-before.log`).
The first failing combination is `reloads` 0, `extra` false, and `answered` false: event capacity 5, length 4, 1 free slot, and 2 outstanding requests.

The probe `raw/case-1-capacity-probe.zig` repeats the setup of case 1 on the base engine without the floor expectation, and `raw/case-1-capacity-probe.log` records it with exit status 0.
On the base, `destroyDocument` returns `OutOfMemory` in 12 of the 196 combinations.
An example is `reloads` 0 with `extra` true: event capacity 5, length 5, 0 free slots, and 1 outstanding request.
The floor expectation fails in 20 combinations, and those 20 include all 12 `OutOfMemory` combinations.
The frozen case therefore cannot reach `OutOfMemory` on the base, because its floor check fails first.

The worker reported this observation to the integrator.
The integrator decided to keep case 1 exactly as frozen, because the floor failure and the `OutOfMemory` destroy are two consequences of the same missing reservation, and the case fails before the change as the contract requires.
Case 1 is unchanged, and it passes on the final tree (`raw/tests-after.log`).

### Case 5 pump loop

The pump loop of case 5 never changed `engine.inputs.buffer.len`, because its per-repetition expectation held in both `raw/tests-before.log` and `raw/tests-after.log`.

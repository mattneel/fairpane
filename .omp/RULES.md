# Fairpane invariants

Preserve the gift-to-humanity mission and full browser scope.
Do not trade correctness for token cost, a deadline, or an impressive demo.
Do not ship another renderer or JavaScript engine behind a Fairpane API.
Do not weaken the test denominator or thresholds to pass a patch.
Do not claim that bootstrap checks establish browser completion.
Do not claim unattended progress outside an active execution environment.
Keep the next executable action in the repository at session boundaries.
